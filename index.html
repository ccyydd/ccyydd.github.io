Ph.D. Student · Tsinghua University

Google ScholarGitHub

I am a third-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Civil Engineering at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Jiansheng Fan. I received my bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2024.

My research focuses on Physical AI and embodied manipulation, particularly physical world models, physical reasoning, and tactile manipulation. I aim to advance AI from understanding the digital world to autonomously interacting with the real physical world.

Feel free to reach out at cyang203912@163.com. I’m always happy to discuss research ideas and potential collaborations!
